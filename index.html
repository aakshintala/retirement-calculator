Stress Tests

Replay infamous market decades to see how your plan fares when history rhymes. Each test grafts the listed return path onto your plan starting today and marches forward year by year (contributions continue until your retirement age, then withdrawals begin). It does not assume those years happen at retirement; it answers “what if the next decade looked like 2000–2009?”
